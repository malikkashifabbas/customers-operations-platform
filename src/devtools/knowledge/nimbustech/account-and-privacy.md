# Account and Privacy Policy — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 March 2026 · Version 3

## 1. Your account

Your NimbusTech account is identified by your mobile phone number. An email address is **required**, because licence keys and invoices are sent by email.

## 2. Changing your details

| Detail | How | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Support ticket with a photo of your CNIC | 2 business days |
| Email address | Support ticket; both the old and new email must confirm | 1 business day |
| Phone number | Support ticket with CNIC and a selfie for verification | 3 business days |

The support assistant can read your details but cannot change them.

## 3. Two-factor authentication and locked accounts

Accounts that have bought a laptop or phone must use two-factor authentication. After **3 failed login attempts** the account is locked for **1 hour**. Support staff can unlock it earlier only after a video identity check.

## 4. Data retention

- Orders, invoices and warranty records: **10 years** (warranty and tax reasons).
- Device serial numbers linked to your account: for as long as the account exists.
- Support tickets: **3 years**.
- Chat assistant conversation memory: **24 hours** after your last message.

## 5. Deleting your account

Deletion requests are completed within **45 days**. Warranty registrations are lost when the account is deleted, so we recommend downloading your invoices first. Unused store credit is lost.

## 6. Data sharing

We share your details only with couriers, payment providers, the installment partner bank (for installment orders) and manufacturers (for warranty claims). We never sell personal data.
