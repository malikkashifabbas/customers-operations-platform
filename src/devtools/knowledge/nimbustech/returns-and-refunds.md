# Returns and Refunds Policy — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Version 6 · Applies to all purchases from the NimbusTech online store.

NimbusTech sells laptops, phones, tablets, monitors, accessories and software licences. Electronics lose value quickly once opened, so our rules depend on whether the box was opened and whether the device is defective. When rules conflict, the rule for the specific product type wins.

## 1. The return window

You can return most products within **14 calendar days from delivery**. There is no extension for weekends or holidays: day 14 is the last day.

Products bought during our **holiday season (15 November – 31 December)** have an extended window: they can be returned until **15 January** of the next year, or 14 days from delivery, whichever is later.

## 2. Unopened vs opened products

| Product state | Can it be returned? | Restocking fee |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened, factory seal intact | Yes, within the window | None |
| Opened, working, all accessories included | Yes, within the window | **15% of the product price** |
| Opened, missing accessories or box | Yes, within the window | 15% plus the price of the missing parts |
| Defective (does not work as described) | Yes, see section 4 | None |

## 3. Products that cannot be returned

- **Activated phones and tablets** (a SIM card was inserted or the device was set up with an account) cannot be returned for change of mind. They can only be returned if they are dead on arrival (section 4).
- **Software licences and digital codes** once the code has been revealed.
- **Opened printer ink and toner.**
- **Custom-configured laptops** (built to your chosen specification) — unless defective.

## 4. Defective and dead-on-arrival products

- **Dead on arrival (DOA):** if a product does not power on or has a major fault, report it **within 7 days of delivery**. We replace it with a new unit; if none is in stock, we refund in full. Activated phones are covered by this rule.
- **Defect found after 7 days:** it is handled under the manufacturer warranty (see the Warranty Policy), not as a return.

NimbusTech pays return shipping for DOA and defective products. For change-of-mind returns the customer pays return shipping; our prepaid return label costs **USD 8.00** and is deducted from the refund.

## 5. How refunds are paid

| How you paid | Refund method | Time after the return passes inspection |
|---|---|---|
| Card | Back to the same card | 3–5 business days |
| PayPal | Back to PayPal | 1–2 business days |
| Bank transfer | Back to the paying bank account | 5–7 business days |
| Installment plan | The plan is cancelled; instalments already paid are refunded to the card | 7–10 business days |

**Store credit option:** instead of a refund you can choose NimbusTech store credit worth **105% of the refund amount** (a 5% bonus). Store credit cannot be turned back into money later.

Inspection takes up to **3 business days** after the product reaches our returns centre.

## 6. Refunds that need approval

Refunds that are not part of a normal return — for example duplicate charges, price-match claims or goodwill refunds — are only paid after a staff member approves them. The support assistant can explain this policy but cannot approve a refund.

## 7. Price drops

If the price of a product you bought drops within **7 days of delivery**, you can ask for the difference as store credit (not money). This does not apply to Black Friday or holiday-season prices.
