# Shipping Policy — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 May 2026 · Version 3

"Business days" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. All times are Pakistan Standard Time (PKT).

## 1. Where we ship

We ship within Pakistan and to the **United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia**. We do not ship to any other country. We do not ship to PO boxes or parcel lockers.

## 2. Shipping times and costs

| Destination | Standard | Cost | Express | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pakistan, major cities | 2–4 business days | Free over USD 99.00, otherwise USD 6.00 | Next business day | USD 14.99 |
| Pakistan, other areas | 4–7 business days | Free over USD 99.00, otherwise USD 9.00 | 2 business days | USD 19.99 |
| UAE and Saudi Arabia | 5–8 business days | USD 25.00 (never free) | Not available | — |

Express orders must be placed before **12:00 noon PKT** on a business day.

International orders may be charged customs duties and VAT by the destination country. These are paid by the customer on delivery and are **not refunded** if the order is returned.

## 3. Signature and ID checks

- Orders worth **more than USD 500.00** need the customer's signature on delivery. The courier will not leave them with a neighbour or guard.
- Orders containing a **phone or tablet** require the customer to show a CNIC (or passport for international orders) matching the name on the order.

## 4. Failed deliveries

The courier tries **three times**. After the third failed attempt the parcel returns to our warehouse and the order is refunded **minus the original shipping cost and a USD 5.00 handling fee**.

## 5. Lost or damaged in transit

If tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, open a support ticket. We investigate with the courier for up to 7 business days, then send a replacement or a refund. Parcels that arrive damaged must be refused at the door or reported within **24 hours** with photos of the box and the product.

## 6. Order processing

Orders paid by card or PayPal are dispatched within 1 business day. Orders paid by bank transfer are dispatched only after the transfer arrives (see the Payments Policy). Custom-configured laptops take **5 extra business days** to build before dispatch.
