# Contact and Support Hours — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 June 2026 · Version 4

## 1. Support hours

| Day | Hours (PKT) |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 10:00 am – 7:00 pm |
| Saturday | 10:00 am – 2:00 pm |
| Sunday | Closed |

There are no special Ramadan hours. Support is closed on public holidays and for the first **two days** of each Eid.

## 2. How to reach us

- **Support tickets:** staff reply within **12 business hours**.
- **Email:** help@nimbustech.example — replies within 1 business day.
- **Phone:** +92-42-0000-5678, Monday to Friday only (not on Saturdays).
- **Repair centre:** Lahore, by appointment only for warranty repairs (book through a support ticket).

## 3. Business customers

Companies buying 10 or more devices can request a business account with invoice payment terms. Business requests are handled by the sales team, not by customer support.

## 4. Escalations

Ask for an escalation in your ticket. A senior agent reviews escalations within **2 business days**.
