# Payments and Installments Policy — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Version 5

## 1. Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards.
- PayPal.
- Bank transfer, only for orders of **USD 1,000.00 or more**.
- Installment plans through our partner bank (section 4).
- NimbusTech store credit.

**NimbusTech does not offer Cash on Delivery (COD)** for any order, because of the high value of electronics. Mobile wallets (JazzCash, Easypaisa) are also not accepted.

## 2. Bank transfer orders

When you choose bank transfer, the order is reserved for **72 hours**. The full amount must reach our account within 72 hours, with your order number in the payment reference. If it does not arrive in time, the order is cancelled automatically and the reserved stock is released. Partial transfers are returned within 5 business days and the order is cancelled.

## 3. Why a payment can fail

| Failure reason | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Card declined by bank | The bank refused the payment | Contact your bank or use another method |
| Address verification failed | The billing address does not match the card | Correct the billing address and order again |
| PayPal payment not completed | The PayPal window was closed early | Place a new order |
| Bank transfer not received in 72 hours | See section 2 | Place a new order |

Failed orders cannot be re-paid; place a new order. You are not charged for a failed order. Temporary bank holds are released within **5 business days**.

## 4. Installment plans

Installments are available on orders between **USD 300.00 and USD 5,000.00**, for cards issued by Pakistani banks only.

| Plan | Fee |
|---|---|
| 3 months | 0% (no fee) |
| 6 months | 2.5% of the order value |
| 12 months | 6% of the order value |

The first instalment is charged at checkout. If two instalments in a row fail, the partner bank — not NimbusTech — contacts you, and the remaining balance may become due at once. Installment orders cannot be paid off early through NimbusTech; contact the partner bank.

## 5. Duplicate charges and refunds

Duplicate charges are refunded after a staff member checks them; all refunds outside a normal return need staff approval.

## 6. Security

NimbusTech never asks for your card PIN, full card number, CVV or OTP by phone, chat, email or SMS.
