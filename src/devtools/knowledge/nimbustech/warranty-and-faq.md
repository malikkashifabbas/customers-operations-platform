# Warranty Policy and FAQ — NimbusTech

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Version 2

## 1. Manufacturer warranty

All new products come with a manufacturer warranty that NimbusTech handles for you:

| Product | Warranty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops | **1 year** | Battery covered for 6 months only |
| Phones and tablets | **1 year** | Battery covered for 6 months only |
| Monitors | **2 years** | Up to 3 dead pixels is not a defect |
| Accessories (chargers, mice, keyboards, cables) | **6 months** | — |
| Refurbished products | **3 months** | Any category |

The warranty starts on the **delivery date** and covers manufacturing defects only.

## 2. What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage: drops, cracked screens, liquid damage.
- Damage from unofficial chargers.
- Devices opened or repaired by anyone other than NimbusTech or the manufacturer — this **voids the warranty completely**.
- Software problems, viruses and lost data. Back up your data before sending a device for repair; we are not responsible for data loss.

## 3. NimbusCare extended warranty

You can buy **NimbusCare** within **30 days of delivery**. It extends the warranty by 2 years and adds accidental-damage cover (one claim per year, with a USD 49.00 service fee per claim). NimbusCare costs 12% of the product price and is non-refundable after 30 days.

## 4. How to make a warranty claim

Open a support ticket with the serial number and a description of the problem. Repairs take **10–15 business days**. If the same defect is repaired three times within the warranty period, we replace the product.

## 5. Frequently asked questions

**Can I return a phone I already set up?**
Only if it is dead on arrival and you report it within 7 days of delivery. Otherwise activated phones cannot be returned.

**Do you have cash on delivery?**
No. Pay by card, PayPal, bank transfer (orders of USD 1,000.00 or more) or an installment plan.

**Is there a fee for 3-month installments?**
No, the 3-month plan has no fee. Six months costs 2.5% and twelve months 6%.
