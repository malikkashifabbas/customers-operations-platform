# Payments Policy — Demo Company

Effective: 1 September 2026 · Version 4

## 1. Accepted payment methods

- Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards (3-D Secure required).
- JazzCash and Easypaisa mobile wallets.
- Cash on Delivery (COD), only for orders of **USD 200.00 or less**. Orders above USD 200.00 must be paid in advance.
- Demo Company store credit, which can be combined with any other method.

We do not accept American Express, PayPal, bank transfer or cryptocurrency for purchases.

## 2. Currency

All prices are shown and charged in US dollars (USD). If your card is issued in Pakistani rupees, your bank converts the amount using its own rate and may add a foreign transaction fee; Demo Company does not control or refund that fee.

## 3. Why a payment can fail

A failed order in your account means we did not receive the money. The most common reasons are:

| Failure reason shown | What it usually means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Card declined by bank | Your bank refused the charge (limit, blocked for online use, suspected fraud) | Call your bank, or pay with another card or wallet |
| 3-D Secure not completed | The OTP step was not finished within 5 minutes | Place the order again and complete the OTP |
| Insufficient wallet balance | The JazzCash/Easypaisa wallet did not have enough money | Top up the wallet and place a new order |
| Payment timed out | The payment page was left open too long | Place a new order |

**A failed order cannot be "retried" or "re-paid".** You must place a new order. The failed order stays in your history with status "failed" and you are not charged for it.

## 4. Money taken but order failed

Sometimes your bank shows a pending amount even though the order failed. This is an authorisation hold, not a real charge. It is released automatically by your bank within **3–7 business days**. If it is still shown after 7 business days, open a support ticket with a screenshot of your bank statement.

## 5. Duplicate charges

If you were charged twice for the same order, open a support ticket. A duplicate charge is refunded only after a staff member has checked it against our payment records; refunds without a return always need staff approval. Once approved, card refunds take 5–7 business days.

## 6. Pending orders

An order can stay "pending" for up to **24 hours** while a wallet or bank payment is confirmed. If the payment is not confirmed within 24 hours, the order is cancelled automatically and any hold is released.

## 7. Security

Demo Company will never ask you for your card PIN, your full card number, your CVV or a one-time password (OTP) by phone, email, chat or SMS. If someone asks for these in our name, do not share them and report it to the support team.
