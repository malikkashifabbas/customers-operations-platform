# Delivery Policy — Demo Company

Effective: 1 July 2026 · Version 2

This policy covers where we deliver, how long delivery takes, what it costs and what happens when a delivery fails. All times are in Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) and "business days" means Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## 1. Where we deliver

We deliver to every city in Pakistan served by our courier partners. We do **not** deliver outside Pakistan. We do not deliver to PO boxes or to military cantonment addresses that do not accept private couriers.

## 2. Delivery times

| Area | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi | 2–3 business days | Next business day |
| Other cities | 4–6 business days | 2 business days |
| Remote areas (northern areas, interior Balochistan, Azad Kashmir) | 8–12 business days | Not available |

Express orders must be placed **before 2:00 pm PKT** to count from that day. Orders placed after 2:00 pm, on Sundays or on public holidays are processed from the next business day.

Furniture and items over 15 kg are delivered by a separate freight partner in **7–10 business days** to all areas, and express delivery is not available for them.

During sale events (for example 11.11 and White Friday) standard delivery may take up to 3 extra business days.

## 3. Delivery charges

- Standard delivery is **free on orders of USD 50.00 or more** (after discounts). Below that, standard delivery costs **USD 4.99**.
- Express delivery always costs **USD 9.99**, even on orders over USD 50.00.
- Freight delivery for furniture costs **USD 19.00** per order, regardless of order value.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) orders have an extra **USD 1.00** handling fee.

## 4. Tracking

You receive an SMS with your tracking number when the order is dispatched. Tracking usually starts showing movement within 24 hours of dispatch.

## 5. Failed deliveries

The courier makes **two delivery attempts**. If both fail (for example nobody is home or the phone is switched off), the parcel goes back to our warehouse.

- Prepaid orders: we contact you to re-deliver. Re-delivery costs **USD 3.00**. If we cannot reach you within 7 days, the order is cancelled and refunded minus the delivery charge.
- COD orders: the order is cancelled automatically. Customers with **three refused or failed COD deliveries in 6 months** lose the COD option and must pay in advance for future orders.

## 6. Damaged parcels

Do not accept a parcel whose outer packaging is clearly torn or crushed. If you notice damage only after opening, report it within 48 hours with photos, as described in the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## 7. Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address only **before the order is dispatched**. After dispatch the address cannot be changed by our team; you can ask the courier directly, but this is not guaranteed.
