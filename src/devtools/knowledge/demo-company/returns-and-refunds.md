# Returns and Refunds Policy — Demo Company

Effective: 1 September 2026 · Version 3 · Applies to all orders placed on the Demo Company online store.

Demo Company sells clothing, shoes, bags, jewellery and home decor. This policy explains when an item can be returned or exchanged, how refunds are paid, and how long each step takes. If two rules seem to conflict, the more specific rule (for example the rule for a product category) wins over the general rule.

## 1. The return window

You can return most items within **30 calendar days from the day the parcel was delivered** to you. The window is counted from the delivery date shown in your delivery confirmation, not from the day you placed the order or the day you paid.

**Transition rule for older orders.** Until 31 August 2026 the return window was 45 days. Orders that were **delivered on or before 31 August 2026** still have the old 45-day window. Orders delivered on or after 1 September 2026 have the new 30-day window, even if they were placed in August.

If the last day of your window falls on a Sunday or a public holiday in Pakistan, the window is extended to the next working day.

## 2. Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging, with all tags still attached. Shoes must be returned in the original shoe box; a shoe box with writing or tape on it is still accepted. Items that show signs of wear, perfume, make-up or pet hair are refused and sent back to you at your cost.

## 3. Items that cannot be returned

The following items can never be returned or refunded, unless they arrived damaged or wrong (see section 5):

- Underwear, swimwear and socks, for hygiene reasons.
- Pierced jewellery such as earrings and nose pins.
- Custom-made or personalised items (for example monogrammed bags or made-to-measure curtains).
- Gift cards and e-vouchers.

## 4. Sale items and "Final Sale"

Items bought at a discount of **up to 40%** follow the normal 30-day return rules.

Items marked **"Final Sale"** (usually discounts above 40%, and all clearance items) **cannot be returned for a refund**. They can only be **exchanged for a different size of the same item within 7 days of delivery**, and only if that size is in stock. If the size is not in stock, no refund is given; you may instead receive store credit for the item's price.

## 5. Damaged, faulty or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, report it **within 48 hours of delivery** with at least two clear photos. In that case:

- Any item can be returned, including items from section 3 and Final Sale items.
- Demo Company pays the return shipping.
- You can choose a replacement or a full refund.

Reports made after 48 hours are handled as a normal return under sections 1–4.

## 6. Return shipping costs

For normal returns (change of mind, wrong size) the customer pays the return shipping. A flat return pickup fee of **USD 3.00** is deducted from the refund if you use our courier pickup. If you drop the parcel at a partner courier office yourself, there is no fee.

**Furniture and large home decor** (any single item over 15 kg) has a **10% restocking fee** on change-of-mind returns. There is no restocking fee on any other category.

## 7. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the **original payment method**:

| How you paid | How you get the money back | How long after we receive the return |
|---|---|---|
| Debit or credit card | Back to the same card | 5–7 business days |
| JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet | Back to the same wallet | 2–3 business days |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Bank transfer to an account in the customer's own name | 10 business days |
| Store credit | Added to your Demo Company account | Same day |

For COD refunds you must provide a bank account (IBAN) in your own name; we cannot transfer to a third person's account. Delivery charges are refunded only when the whole order is returned because it was damaged, faulty or wrong.

A refund is only started after the returned item has reached our warehouse and passed inspection. Inspection usually takes 2 business days.

## 8. Refunds without a return

Some refunds do not need a return, for example a duplicate charge or an order that was cancelled before dispatch. Every refund that is not part of a normal return must be **approved by a member of staff** before it is paid. Our support assistant can explain the policy but cannot issue a refund by itself.

## 9. Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until it is dispatched. After dispatch the order cannot be cancelled; you must wait for delivery and then return it under this policy.
