# Warranty and Frequently Asked Questions — Demo Company

Effective: 1 September 2026 · Version 1

## 1. Product warranty

Demo Company is a fashion and home store, so most products do not carry a manufacturer's warranty. Our own guarantee covers manufacturing defects only:

| Category | Guarantee | What is covered | What is not covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clothing | No guarantee after the return window | — | Normal wear, washing damage |
| Shoes | **3 months** from delivery | Sole coming apart, broken heel, stitching failure | Worn-out soles, water damage |
| Bags | **6 months** from delivery | Broken zip, strap or buckle | Scratches, stains |
| Home decor (non-furniture) | **6 months** from delivery | Manufacturing defects | Breakage from falls |
| Furniture | **12 months** from delivery | Structural defects (frame, joints) | Fabric wear, sun fading |

To claim the guarantee, open a support ticket with photos and your order number. If the claim is accepted, we repair or replace the item; refunds under the guarantee are given only if repair and replacement are both impossible.

## 2. Frequently asked questions

**Can I return something I bought on sale?**
Yes, if the discount was 40% or less: normal 30-day rules. If it was marked "Final Sale", no refund — only a size exchange within 7 days.

**I paid cash on delivery. How do I get my refund?**
By bank transfer to an account in your own name, within 10 business days after the return reaches our warehouse.

**My order failed but money left my account.**
That is a temporary bank hold. It disappears within 3–7 business days.

**Do you deliver on Sundays?**
No. Deliveries happen Monday to Saturday.
