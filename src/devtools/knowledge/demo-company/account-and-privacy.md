# Account and Privacy Policy — Demo Company

Effective: 15 June 2026 · Version 2

## 1. Your account and identity

Your account is identified by your **mobile phone number**. Your email address is optional and is used only for receipts and order updates.

## 2. Changing your details

| Detail | How to change it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Open a support ticket | Changed within 1 business day |
| Email address | Open a support ticket | We send a confirmation link to the new email |
| Phone number | Not possible by chat or ticket | Because the phone number is your identity, you must visit a partner store with your CNIC, or create a new account |
| Delivery addresses | Before checkout, in each order | Cannot be changed after dispatch (see Delivery Policy) |

The support assistant can show you the details we have on file, but it cannot change them.

## 3. Locked accounts

After **5 failed login attempts** your account is locked for **30 minutes**. It unlocks automatically; support staff cannot unlock it earlier. If it locks again, you can reset your password from the login screen.

## 4. What data we keep and for how long

- Order and payment records: **7 years**, because tax law requires it.
- Support chats and tickets: **2 years**.
- Chat assistant conversation memory: **24 hours** after your last message, then it is deleted automatically.
- Marketing preferences: until you change them.

## 5. Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted by opening a support ticket. We complete deletion within **30 days**. Order and payment records are kept for the legally required 7 years even after deletion, but they are no longer linked to a usable account. Store credit is lost when an account is deleted.

## 6. Sharing your data

We share your name, phone number and address only with courier partners to deliver your orders, and with payment providers to process payments. We never sell your data. Another customer can never see your orders or details, even if they know your email or phone number.

## 7. Marketing messages

You can stop promotional SMS by replying STOP. Order updates (dispatch, delivery, refunds) are always sent and cannot be turned off.
