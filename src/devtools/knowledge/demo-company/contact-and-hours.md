# Contact and Support Hours — Demo Company

Effective: 1 October 2026 · Version 5

## 1. Support hours

| Day | Hours (PKT) |
|---|---|
| Monday to Saturday | 9:00 am – 6:00 pm |
| Sunday | Closed |
| Public holidays | Closed |

**Ramadan hours:** during Ramadan, support works Monday to Saturday from **10:00 am to 4:00 pm**.

**Eid:** support is closed for the first **three days** of Eid ul-Fitr and Eid ul-Adha.

The chat assistant is available 24 hours a day, but staff decisions (refund approvals, profile changes) only happen during support hours.

## 2. How to reach us

- **Support tickets** (recommended): open a ticket from your account. Staff reply within **24 business hours**.
- **Email:** support@demo-company.example — replies within 2 business days.
- **Phone:** +92-21-0000-1234, during support hours only.

There is no WhatsApp support line. Messages claiming to be Demo Company on WhatsApp are not from us.

## 3. Complaints

If you are not happy with how your issue was handled, ask for it to be escalated in your ticket. A team lead reviews escalated tickets within **3 business days**.

## 4. Head office

Demo Company does not accept walk-in customers at its head office. Returns must go through the courier process in the Returns and Refunds Policy.
